---
title: Scaled and zoomed grids
description: A grid inside a parent scaled by CSS, such as a zoomable canvas, drags and resizes under the pointer with no setting.
---

A grid inside a parent drawn with `transform: scale()` (a zoomable canvas, a preview at half size)
works as it is. At the start of each gesture the engine compares the root's box on screen with its
own size, and divides what the pointer does by that scale: the item stays under the pointer and
lands in the cell it is over.

```tsx title="canvas.tsx"
<div style={{ transform: `scale(${zoom})`, transformOrigin: "0 0" }}>
    <GridLayout.Root>…</GridLayout.Root>
</div>
```

The scale is read again at each gesture, so a zoom that changes between two drags needs nothing.
The grid's own width is measured in its own pixels: a scale does not change its breakpoint.

When the box on screen cannot tell (a scale that changes during a gesture, a transform the box
does not show), give it:

```tsx title="fixed-scale.tsx"
<GridLayout.Root scale={0.5}>…</GridLayout.Root>
```

A drag source's `dragOffset` is on screen, in the page's pixels: the engine divides it by the scale
too.

A rotated or skewed parent is not supported.
